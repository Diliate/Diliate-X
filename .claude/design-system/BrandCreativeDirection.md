# BrandCreativeDirection.md — DILIATE V2

## Moodboard (descriptive — visual references)

Think: a dark cockpit interface meets a product-launch keynote. Deep space blacks and graphites, thin glowing edges of blue/purple light tracing the silhouette of UI elements, glass panels that feel like they're floating a few millimeters above the background rather than sitting flat on it. Reference the _feeling_ of an Apple keynote reveal slide, a Stripe dashboard at night, and Linear's command palette — precise, quiet, confident. Not "cyberpunk neon," not "generic SaaS gradient blob."

## Brand Direction

DILIATE V2 positions as a software company whose own site is proof of engineering craft — the site _is_ the pitch. Every interaction should feel deliberate, like it was tuned by someone who cares about the last 5% of polish most competitors skip.

## Creative Direction

- **Cinematic, not decorative.** Motion and visuals serve storytelling (revealing a product's real value) — never motion for its own sake.
- **Depth through layering, not clutter.** Glassmorphism and glow are used to create spatial hierarchy (what's in front, what's behind), not as a stylistic sticker applied everywhere.
- **Confidence through restraint.** Large type, generous whitespace, one strong focal point per section — the opposite of the old site's dense, agency-brochure layout.

## Brand Personality

Precise. Immersive. Technically credible. Slightly futuristic without tipping into sci-fi cliché. The DILIATE logo (arrow-through-D) already carries a sense of upward momentum/direction — the new visual system should reinforce that idea of forward motion through animated light and directional gradients, not contradict it.

## What This Is NOT

- Not a generic dark-mode SaaS template (gradient blob hero, 3-column feature grid, stock illustration).
- Not the old site's marketing-agency brochure feel.
- Not over-animated to the point of hurting usability or performance.
