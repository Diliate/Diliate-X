## Mission

To build software with the discipline and polish of top-tier product companies, for businesses that don't have access to that caliber of engineering in-house.

## Vision

To be the dev partner known for shipping real, working products — not decks and mockups.

## Brand Voice

Confident, precise, low-hype. Speaks like an engineer who respects the reader's time — no buzzword-stacking ("synergistic AI-powered paradigm shifts"). Claims are backed by specifics (real case studies, real numbers where possible).

## Writing Style

Short, direct sentences. Active voice. Technical accuracy over marketing fluff — if a claim can't be backed up, don't make it.

## Messaging Pillars

1. **Proof over promises** — every capability claim ties back to a real, live product.
2. **Full-stack ownership** — one team/partner across design, engineering, and deployment.
3. **Built to scale** — architecture decisions made for growth, not just an MVP demo.

## Tone

Professional but not corporate-stiff. Direct, occasionally dry — never gimmicky.

## Logo Guidelines

New mark (July 2026), supplied by the project owner: a cleaner arrow-through-"D" icon (`logo-source.png`, black on white as delivered). Production version is recolored to yellow (`logo-yellow.png`, `#FFC700`, transparent background) — this is the version used sitewide. No wordmark variant currently in use (icon-only across nav/footer/welcome screen); revisit if a lockup with "DILIATE" text is needed for contexts where the icon alone isn't identifiable.

## Color Philosophy

Locked for the logo specifically: yellow (`#FFC700`) as a signature brand color, chosen to stand apart from the interface's blue/purple/cyan system rather than blend into it — the logo reads as "the brand" against "the product," a deliberate visual distinction. Broader interface color philosophy remains as specified in `ColorTypographySystem.md`.
