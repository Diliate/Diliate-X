# PRD.md — DILIATE V2

## Business Overview

DILIATE is a software development company brand positioning premium, enterprise-grade custom software services — SaaS, AI applications, CRM/ERP, healthcare platforms, and real estate platforms — built on proof from real, shipped products (WRES, MailEngine Pro, Sharma Pickle Store — CareBridge is unreleased and excluded from public showcase).

## Mission

To deliver software with the polish and engineering discipline of category-leading products (Stripe, Linear, Vercel-tier) to businesses that would otherwise only access it through large agencies or in-house teams they can't afford to build.

## Vision

To become a recognized, credible software development company that clients seek out by name — not a freelancer profile, but a company with a track record.

## Target Audience

- **Primary:** SMBs and startups needing custom SaaS/web apps, sourced via Upwork, Fiverr, LinkedIn, direct outreach.
- **Secondary:** Mid-market and enterprise clients (e.g., Dubai real estate agencies, healthcare orgs) evaluating a dev partner for larger, longer-term engagements.

## Problems We Solve

- Businesses need custom software but can't justify a full in-house engineering team.
- Existing freelance options feel unproven or inconsistent in quality.
- Off-the-shelf SaaS doesn't fit specialized workflows (real estate CRM, hospital logistics, bulk email infra).

## Goals

- Establish DILIATE as a credible, company-level brand (not "hire an individual").
- Convert cold visitors into qualified leads via case studies and a clear services offering.
- Rank organically for relevant service + geography keywords.

## Business Objectives

- Land the first DILIATE-branded client contract within [TBD timeframe].
- Use existing products as case studies to shortcut trust-building.

## User Personas

1. **Startup Founder** — needs an MVP built fast, evaluating cost vs. quality tradeoff.
2. **Real Estate Agency Owner (Dubai)** — needs CRM/lead automation, cares about credibility and local relevance.
3. **Healthcare Operations Lead** — needs a reliable, compliant-feeling platform partner.

## Features / Modules

- Home (value prop, service overview, proof points)
- Services (SaaS, AI, CRM/ERP, Healthcare, Real Estate, Custom Software, Mobile, Cloud)
- Case Studies (WRES, MailEngine Pro, Sharma Pickle Store)
- About / Company
- Contact (lead capture form → Server Action)

## Future Features

- Client portal / project tracking
- Blog (SEO content engine)
- Testimonials once available

## Functional Requirements

- Contact form with validation, spam protection, and email delivery.
- Case study pages with structured, reusable content model (so new case studies are easy to add).

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Lighthouse Performance ≥ 90, Accessibility ≥ 95.
- Mobile-first responsive across all breakpoints.
- Sub-2.5s LCP on 4G.

## Success Metrics

- Organic traffic growth month over month.
- Contact form submission → qualified lead conversion rate.
- Time-on-site / case study engagement.

## Competitive Analysis

Direct competitors: boutique dev agencies targeting Dubai/global SMB market. Differentiator: DILIATE leads with real, live, working products rather than static mockups — this is a defensible advantage and should be the loudest message on the site.

## Project Scope

V2 marketing site with case studies and contact flow, built on Next.js, deployed to Vercel.

## Out of Scope (V2)

- Client portal / authenticated areas
- Blog/CMS (planned for later phase)
- Payment processing

## Acceptance Criteria

- All core pages live and responsive.
- Contact form functional end-to-end (test submission received).
- Lighthouse targets met on production build.
- No placeholder/lorem ipsum content in final version.
